About MashTech Repairs

MashTech Repair is a technology repair business based in Pretoria,Gauteng.The busines provides reliable,efficient and affordable repair services for a variety of everyday technology devices.

Our goal is to make technology repair services easier and more accesible for students,local residents and other customers who depend on their devices for everyday activities.

Our Backgroud

MashTech Repairs was established as a local technology repair service focused on helping customers solve common device problems. The business provides support for smartphones, laptops, tablets and gaming consoles.

By providing convenient local repair services, MashTech Repairs aims to build long-term relationships with customers and become a trusted technology repair provider in Pretoria.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of the most trusted local technology repair providers in Pretoria by delivering dependable services and building strong relationships with our customers.

Our values

  • Reliability: We aim to provide dependable repair services.
