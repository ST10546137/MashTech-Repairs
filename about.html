About MashTech Repairs

MashTech Repairs is a local technology repair business concept based in Pretoria, Gauteng. The website is designed to make information about repair services and customer enquiries easy to find.

Our aim is to help students, local residents and other customers who rely on their devices for study, work and everyday communication.
